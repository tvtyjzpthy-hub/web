Beppu — Jigoku Meguri
The hell pools
Eight geothermal pools — vivid blues, reds, grey mud. Each dramatically different. Drive 45 min from Yufuin. Allow 2–3 hours to see the main ones. Umi Jigoku (cobalt blue) and Chi no Ike Jigoku (blood red) are the standouts.

Driving in Japan

Left-hand drive — same side as the UK, so no surprises there.

ETC card — if the hire car has one, use it for toll roads (faster lanes). If not, keep small change for toll booths.

Google Maps or Apple Maps — both work well in Japan. Download the Yufuin area offline before you leave Fukuoka in case signal is patchy in the mountains.

Fuel — return the car full. Fuel up within 5km of Hakata Station before drop-off. Keep the receipt.

Resort payment — JPY 407,807 due on arrival. Confirm card acceptance with the resort beforehand; have yen as backup.

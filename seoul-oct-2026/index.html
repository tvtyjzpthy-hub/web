Transport in Seoul

Kakao T (not KakaoTaxi — that's the old name) — download before you land. English mode available in settings. Works with a foreign Mastercard/Visa. Every taxi in this plan is under ₩20,000 (~£11).

Uber also works in Seoul as a backup if Kakao T defeats you on day one.

Airport → hotel: direct taxi recommended with luggage. ~₩80,000–90,000 from Incheon T2, ~70 min. Tell them Grand Hyatt Seoul, Hannam-dong (한남동).

Kakao Maps — more accurate than Apple Maps for Seoul walking routes and transit. Worth having both.
